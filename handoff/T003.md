## Summary
Clean national tables of every mayoral race in the 2020 and 2024 Romanian local elections have been built and saved to Parquet under `data/processed/`. The dataset covers exactly 3,187 administrative units (UATs) per election year, matching the national UAT reference table with a 100.0% match rate, including the General Mayor of Bucharest (`siruta` 179132) and the six Bucharest sector mayors (`siruta` 179141–179196). In 2020, the official AEP database recorded candidate names only for independent candidates, so party nominees are recorded with `candidate_name: null` as instructed (with backlog task `T003c` added). Tied races resolved in Round 2 runoffs (Balaci, Rebra, Orțișoara in 2020; Poșaga, Chețani in 2024) and later-date rerun elections (Moldova Nouă in 2020, Costinești in 2024) are fully accounted for, with deciding-round figures in the races table. All data in `data/processed/` is authentic official election data; synthetic data is strictly confined to `tests/`.

- **Dates stored as date32:** `election_date`, `term_start`, and `planned_term_end` are stored as PyArrow `date32` objects, as amended by user guidance.
- **Alliance members field:** In `config/party_aliases.yaml`, only alliances have `members: []` (empty list for manual enrichment), whereas non-alliances omit the key, complying with the user amendment.
