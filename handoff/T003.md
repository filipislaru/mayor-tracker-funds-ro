## Summary
Clean national tables of every mayoral race in the 2020 and 2024 Romanian local elections have been built and saved to Parquet under `data/processed/`. The dataset covers exactly 3,187 administrative units (UATs) per election year, matching the national UAT reference table with a 100.0% match rate, including the General Mayor of Bucharest (`siruta` 179132) and the six Bucharest sector mayors (`siruta` 179141–179196). In 2020, the official AEP database recorded candidate names only for independent candidates, so party nominees are recorded with `candidate_name: null` as instructed (with backlog task `T003c` added). Tied races resolved in Round 2 runoffs (Balaci, Rebra, Orțișoara in 2020; Poșaga, Chețani in 2024) and later-date rerun elections (Moldova Nouă in 2020, Costinești in 2024) are fully accounted for, with deciding-round figures in the races table. All rerun UATs, dates, and administrative code remappings are managed via `config/election_overrides.yaml` with evidence notes, and tied races are derived dynamically from the official `bd_p` runoff files. All data in `data/processed/` is authentic official election data; synthetic data is strictly confined to `tests/`.

### Detailed Findings from the 2020 Discrepancy Investigation

1. **Party name spelling variants:**
   - **None of the 23 discrepancies are spelling variants.** Every party name in the table matches character-for-character, diacritic-for-diacritic between `bd_sectii` and `pv_final_cntry_p.csv`.
2. **Later-date or corrected races in Constanța, Galați, Maramureș, Mureș:**
   - **None of Constanța, Galați, Maramureș, or Mureș point to another later-date or corrected race.**
   - In **Galați, Maramureș, and Mureș**, the raw votes in `bd_sectii_27.09.2020+24.01.2021.csv` match `pv_final_cntry_p.csv` to the exact single vote (Galați: 4,577 vs 4,577; Maramureș: 415 vs 415; Mureș: 3,464 vs 3,464). The discrepancies were 100% artifacts of the reconciliation function checking `or "independent" in pname_clean.lower()`.
