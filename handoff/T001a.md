## Summary
Two safety gaps identified during T001 review have been closed in `mayortracker.util.http.PoliteClient`. A configurable `http.max_retry_after_seconds` (default 300) cap was added to `config/settings.yaml` and the `HttpSettings` model, causing responses requesting excessive delays to immediately raise `HttpGiveUp` with a descriptive message rather than sleeping. An explicit host allow-list (`allowed_hosts: set[str] | None`) was introduced on `get()` and `download()`, strictly preventing cross-host redirects unless explicitly permitted, while exempting `robots.txt` resolution per RFC 9309 §2.3.1.2. All 67 tests pass and linter checks pass with zero warnings.
