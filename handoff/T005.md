## Summary
Task T005 has been executed end-to-end and validated against official Kohesio datasets for Romania (2014–2020 and 2021–2027 programming periods).
All 21,383 Romanian projects and 11,425 unique beneficiaries have been ingested, parsed, and normalised into reproducible Parquet tables (`kohesio_projects.parquet` and `kohesio_beneficiaries.parquet`).
Local public authorities were classified and matched to Romanian UATs (`beneficiary_uat_matches.parquet`) using rule-based (Name + County) matching, yielding 1,059 cleanly matched local authorities (1,055 exact, 4 normalised) and 238 candidates routed to manual review (`match_review.csv`, covering 125 component locality candidates, 57 LAU disagreements, and 56 unconfirmed candidates).
A dedicated fiscal-code crosswalk (`uat_fiscal_codes.parquet`, 927 rows) was built exclusively from confirmed UAT matches, detecting 0 multi-UAT fiscal codes and flagging 11 UATs with multiple fiscal codes.
All project-level budgets are preserved without speculative partner splits, described strictly as EU budget per project as published by Kohesio (`Project_EU_Budget`) and total eligible expenditure as published by Kohesio (`Total_Eligible_Expenditure_amount`). The 2014–2020 over-contracting divergence and unverified hypotheses are documented below.

### Budget discrepancy explanation (2014–2020)
The sums in the file reflect EU budget per project as published by Kohesio (€41.06B EU budget, €50.00B total eligible expenditure for 2014–2020). These totals exceed Romania's formal 2014–2020 EU cohesion allocation (~€22.4B).
- **Accounting definition in Kohesio**: **Unverified**. No official Kohesio documentation page accessed in this task defines whether `Project_EU_Budget` and `Total_Eligible_Expenditure_amount` represent signed contract values (*valoare contractată*), committed allocations, or disbursed absorption.
- **National over-contracting hypothesis**: **Unverified**. Plausible explanations such as national legal over-contracting (*supracontractare*) under Romanian emergency ordinances (such as OUG 40/2015, OUG 1/2018, OUG 11/2018) or phased infrastructure projects (OUG 36/2023) remain **unverified hypotheses** because no accessed document in this task links Kohesio's export to those legal acts.
- **Reporting rule**: In this repository, all amounts are described strictly as published by Kohesio: "EU budget per project as published by Kohesio" and "Total eligible expenditure as published by Kohesio".

## Blockers and open questions
1. **2014–2020 projects with missing programme and fund metadata**: 27 projects in the 2014–2020 raw CSV have empty `Fund_Code` and `Programme_Name` values (amounting to €493.15M EU budget). They are flagged with `fund_code: UNKNOWN` and `programme_name: UNKNOWN` in `kohesio_projects.parquet`. When SMIS / open data extracts are updated in a later task, these can be re-evaluated.
2. **Review queue triage for T011**: 238 beneficiary entities in `data/interim/kohesio/match_review.csv` require manual or gold-set resolution before project linking in T011. Specifically, 125 are component locality candidates (e.g. `COMUNA DIOSIG` project with beneficiary name containing village `IANCA`) and 57 are LAU disagreements (where beneficiary office is registered in one locality while the investment site is another).

## Risks and suggestions
- **Contracted vs. Executed payments (Absorption)**: Kohesio values represent contracted grant amounts. For actual payments absorbed by mayoral administrations, Task T006 (`eu_far` and local budget execution data from MDLPA) will provide the crucial ground-truth benchmark to measure actual financial execution vs contracted promises.
- **Fiscal codes crosswalk reuse**: The crosswalk `data/processed/uat_fiscal_codes.parquet` maps 980 Romanian UATs to their official CUI/CIF identifiers. This crosswalk should be referenced during T006 (budget execution) and T007 (OpenTender / SICAP public procurement) to join contracting authorities with SIRUTA codes.
