## Sources used and version choice
### SIRUTA (`source_id: siruta`)
- **Version chosen**: "SIRUTA_s1 2026" (Semestrul 1 2026).
- **Rationale**:
  - It is the newest official INS release available on `data.gov.ro`.
  - Content check: Genuine UTF-8 text CSV (`efbbbf` UTF-8 BOM, semicolon-delimited) containing all 42 counties (NIV 1), 3,181 standard UATs (NIV 2), 6 Bucharest sectors (NIV 3, TIP 6), and 13,749 component localities and villages (NIV 3).
  - Comparison with alternatives: "SIRUTA_an 2025" on `data.gov.ro` was inspected and found to be an XLSX workbook mislabeled as `.csv` (starts with `PK\x03\x04` zip magic bytes).
- **CSV URL**: `https://data.gov.ro/dataset/721c9059-5f87-4c79-9854-a1d5c18f58d5/resource/dac903f0-32b5-489a-89e7-2c80d96cf68d/download/siruta_s1_2026.csv`
- **Documentation file URL**: `https://data.gov.ro/dataset/06950cb3-41b7-48b8-91bb-4a329786d178/resource/8d5d8a77-d8bf-425d-a9f9-2514d751e84e/download/legenda-siruta.xml`
- **Licence**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (`CC-BY-4.0`), URL `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`.
- **Real file format validation**: `read_siruta_csv()` validates magic bytes before reading (rejecting XLSX/ZIP archives and OLE files), sniffs delimiters (`;` or `,`), strictly decodes with `utf-8-sig`, and normalizes column headers to lowercase.
- **Documentation quote**: Replaced the unverified quote in `pipeline/src/mayortracker/normalise/uat.py` with the exact text and tables extracted from `legenda-siruta.xml`.

## Risks and suggestions
- **Bucharest Sector Boundaries**: Eurostat GISCO LAU does not partition Bucharest into its 6 sectors (`RO_179132` covers all of Bucharest). When building the interactive map in T013, sector polygons should be integrated from ANCPI / geo-spatial.org.
- **Polygon Simplification**: The GeoJSON simplification tolerance of 0.001 degrees produces an efficient 4.43 MB file. As noted in the brief, minor sliver gaps may appear on vector maps, which will be styled appropriately in T013 using TopoJSON or canvas/WebGL boundary rendering.
